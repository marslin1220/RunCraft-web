Common questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. iPhone can guide the workout on its own. A paired Apple Watch lets you follow progress and use controls on your wrist. The data you can see depends on your devices and the permissions you allow.

Do I need a race goal or a VDOT value?

Not always. You can start with run-walk sessions or a free run. Some courses need pace guidance, relevant training experience and enough time to prepare.

Will saving a race replace my current course?

No. Save the goal, then arrange training separately. Your existing course and history stay in place. A new course still needs its own review of the workouts and preparation requirements.

Will poor sleep automatically change my workout?

No. Sleep and heart-rate information may prompt you to check how you feel, but RunCraft will not use it to calculate a replacement workout. Normal-looking data does not mean you are ready to train.

How do I continue after a course or time off?

Choose what you want to do next and check whether your saved running experience still reflects your situation. Before starting another course, review your available time, preparation and recovery.

Where does my data go?

Your devices process the data, and selected records can sync through your private iCloud database. You do not need a RunCraft account. There are no ads or third-party analytics. Diagnostic files go to support only if you choose to share them.
