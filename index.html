Sound familiar?

The problem was never effort

Most runners who stall aren't lazy. They're guessing — about pace, about volume, about whether today should be hard or easy. Here's where the guessing usually shows up.

Every run comes out the same medium-hard

Easy days aren't easy enough to recover from, and hard days aren't fresh enough to be fast. You train constantly and plateau anyway.

The plan you found online wasn't written for you

A PDF from a search result assumes someone else's fitness. Its paces are either a jog or a race, and you can't tell which.

You're adding distance and hoping nothing breaks

The ramp-up feels fine right up until it doesn't. By the time something hurts, the mistake is already three weeks old.

You finish a run with no idea if it was any good

The watch says 8 km in 44 minutes. It doesn't say whether that was the session you were supposed to run.

How RunCraft answers

Every situation, handled

“How fast is my easy pace, really?”

Your paces, from a result you actually ran

Enter one recent race or time trial. RunCraft derives your VDOT fitness score from it and turns that single number into every pace you'll train at — so “easy” has a real number attached, and so does everything else.

  • Easy, Marathon, Threshold, Interval and Repetition paces
  • Honest race predictions from 5K to marathon
  • Paces move as your fitness does — no manual resetting
“It's 6am and I don't want to make a decision.”

Today's session is already waiting

A periodized schedule is built from your race date backwards, so no morning starts with a blank page. Open the app — or just glance at your Lock Screen — and today is already decided.

  • Widgets put today's session one glance away
  • Recovery days adjust when your readiness dips
  • No race on the calendar? Run a simple weekly routine instead
“I can't run 30 minutes without stopping yet.”

Start from walking, not from shame

Not every plan should begin at 5K. RunCraft's walk-run progression starts where you actually are and builds toward 30 minutes non-stop, one manageable step at a time.

  • Alternating walk and run intervals, timed for you
  • Progression paced to weeks, not willpower
  • No Apple Watch needed — the iPhone can guide the whole run
“Am I ramping up too fast?”

Something is watching the ramp for you

RunCraft tracks your acute-to-chronic training load — this week's work against the base you've built — and says something before the jump becomes an injury, not after.

  • Weekly load trend shown as a plain-language banner
  • A warning when the ramp crosses into risk
  • An 80/20 check that keeps easy days honestly easy
“I finished. Was that good?”

An answer, not just a number

Every run is scored against the session it was meant to be — not against a stranger on a leaderboard. You find out whether you hit it, and what it did for your fitness.

  • Per-kilometre splits with pace and heart rate
  • Effort rated against your planned session
  • VDOT progress tracked over months, not days
“My coach gave me a session no app understands.”

Build it once, run it on your wrist

Warmup, reps, recoveries, cooldown — drag them into place and send the whole thing to your Apple Watch with one tap. It runs exactly as written.

  • Warmup, work, recovery and cooldown blocks
  • Distance or time goals per step
  • Pace alerts configured per interval
“That one was worth remembering.”

A card worth posting

Some runs earn a photo. Drop in your own shot and RunCraft lays the stats over it in a story-shaped card — no cluttered dashboard screenshot required.

  • Your photo, your run, clean stats on top
  • Sized for Instagram stories
  • Preview before you post
Apple Watch

Mid-interval is the worst time to do math

“Am I on pace right now?”

Run the session, not the arithmetic

Start today's workout from the watch and RunCraft carries it. You get a target, a tap when you drift off it, and a count of what's left — so your attention stays on running.

  • Live pace target with on-target feedback
  • Heart-rate zone display with haptic alerts when you drift
  • Interval progress — rep count, distance remaining
  • A complication that puts today's session on your watch face
“My phone's in my pocket — is it still following?”

The iPhone mirrors the session live

Pull out your phone mid-run and the same workout is right there — rep counter, live pace, heart-rate zone — in sync with your wrist. Hand it to someone at the finish and they can see exactly where you are.

  • Live rep progress mirrored from the watch
  • Pace, heart-rate zone and cadence at a glance
  • Pause or end the workout from either device
Why it works

Built on VDOT, not vibes

Every pace, every session and every prediction above comes from one place: Jack Daniels' VDOT methodology, the framework coaches have used for decades to turn a single race result into an entire training prescription. RunCraft doesn't invent a training philosophy — it does the arithmetic that has always stood between runners and a real plan, and it does it every day without being asked.
