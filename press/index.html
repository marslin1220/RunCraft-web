One line

RunCraft brings running plans, pace guidance and guided workouts together on iPhone and Apple Watch.

Short — about 60 words

RunCraft helps runners build a routine or prepare for a race with course planning, VDOT-based pace guidance and workouts on iPhone and Apple Watch. Runners review relevant background and course requirements, follow each session, and keep their completed runs. Free download, 21-day full experience, optional Lifetime purchase. No subscription.

Long — about 160 words

RunCraft connects choosing a running course, following a session and reviewing the recorded run in one independent app.

Regular running and race preparation have different requirements. Relevant history and saved inputs provide context; course selection remains a deliberate review. Recovery observations are not automatic workout prescriptions or guarantees of readiness.

Guided workouts run on iPhone and Apple Watch. Runners can review available route, pace, heart-rate and effort information after recording, and build their own structured workouts.

RunCraft is free to download, with a 21-day full experience from first App Store acquisition and an optional Lifetime purchase. There is no subscription. Data is processed on device; selected records can sync through private iCloud.

About Mars

Mars became a runner by invitation — friends signed up for a race and asked him to come along.

Training for it, he kept hitting the same gap. Some apps would generate a plan. Others would faithfully record the run he had just finished. Almost none connected the two and told him what to do next: the plan never reacted to the run, and the run never informed the plan.

RunCraft is the app he wanted during that first training block. He designs, builds, tests and supports it himself, and trains on the plans it produces.

Reach him at support@marstudio.app.
