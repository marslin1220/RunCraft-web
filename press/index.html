One line

RunCraft helps runners build personalized training plans and understand what to do next after every run.

Short — about 60 words

RunCraft helps runners build personalized training plans and understand what to do next after every run. From one recent race result it derives your VDOT fitness score, builds a periodized plan toward your goal, paces each session live on Apple Watch, and scores every finished run against the session it was meant to be. iPhone and Apple Watch. One-time purchase, no subscription.

Long — about 160 words

Running apps tend to do one of two things: generate a training plan, or record the run you just finished. Very few connect the two — which leaves the runner holding the hardest question alone: given how that went, what should I do next?

RunCraft is built to answer it. Enter one recent race result and it derives your VDOT fitness score — the framework coaches have used for decades to turn a single performance into a full set of training paces. From there it builds a periodized plan toward your race date, guides each session live on Apple Watch with pace targets and haptic alerts, watches your training load for ramp-ups that lead to injury, and scores every finished run against the session it was meant to be.

RunCraft is an independent app for iPhone and Apple Watch. One-time purchase, no subscription. Health data is computed on device and synced privately through the runner's own iCloud.

About Mars

Mars became a runner by invitation — friends signed up for a race and asked him to come along.

Training for it, he kept hitting the same gap. Some apps would generate a plan. Others would faithfully record the run he had just finished. Almost none connected the two and told him what to do next: the plan never reacted to the run, and the run never informed the plan.

RunCraft is the app he wanted during that first training block. He designs, builds, tests and supports it himself, and trains on the plans it produces.
